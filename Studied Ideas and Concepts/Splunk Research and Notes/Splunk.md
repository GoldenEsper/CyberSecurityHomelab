# What is it?
Software that is utilised for searching, indexing and analysing generated data. It allows for creation of alerts, dashboards and graphs to respond to events in real time.
## What form factors does it come it?
- On premises and SaaS products
- SIEM (Security Information and Event Management)
- SOAR (Security Orchestration and Response)
